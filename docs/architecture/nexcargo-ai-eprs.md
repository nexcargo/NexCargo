PART I — PRODUCT FOUNDATION

1. Product Vision

NexCargo is a digital logistics marketplace designed to connect cargo owners and transport service providers across Mozambique and the Southern African Development Community (SADC).
The platform creates a trusted environment where businesses can publish transportation needs, discover available logistics capacity, negotiate transport services, manage shipments and complete transactions through secure digital workflows.
By combining intelligent freight matching, shipment visibility, secure financial orchestration and mobile-first technology, NexCargo aims to simplify cargo movement, improve transport utilisation and reduce inefficiencies across regional logistics networks.
The platform is designed to become a digital coordination layer for fragmented freight markets, enabling more efficient collaboration between cargo owners, transport operators, drivers and logistics ecosystem participants.

2. Market Context and Opportunity

The logistics ecosystem in Mozambique and neighbouring SADC markets presents significant opportunities for digital transformation.
Many cargo owners and transport providers continue to rely on fragmented communication channels, informal negotiations and manual processes to coordinate freight movement.
Common challenges include:
•	Limited visibility of available transport capacity 
•	Inefficient freight discovery processes 
•	High levels of empty return kilometres 
•	Difficulty verifying reliable transport providers 
•	Limited shipment tracking capabilities 
•	Manual document exchange 
•	Payment uncertainty between parties 
•	Reduced operational efficiency for smaller logistics operators 
NexCargo addresses these challenges by providing a structured digital marketplace where demand and transport capacity can be connected more efficiently.

3. Product Objectives

NexCargo is designed to achieve the following objectives:

3.1 Improve Freight Market Efficiency

Enable cargo owners to access a wider network of transport providers while allowing transport operators to improve vehicle utilisation and discover additional business opportunities.

3.2 Increase Transport Capacity Utilisation

Reduce unused transport capacity through intelligent matching, improved route visibility and return-load optimisation.

3.3 Build Trust Between Participants

Provide digital identity verification, transparent workflows, secure transaction mechanisms and traceable shipment records.

3.4 Digitise Logistics Operations

Replace fragmented manual processes with integrated workflows for shipment management, documentation, communication and payment coordination.

3.5 Enable Regional Logistics Growth

Create a scalable platform capable of supporting domestic Mozambique logistics and future expansion across SADC transport corridors.
